Schedule recurring messages and event-based reminders in your Discord server. Text, images, role pings, custom timing — set it once and let it run.

Features
🔁
Recurring Schedules
Set messages to fire on any interval — hourly, daily, weekly, or custom timing with fine-grained control.
🖼️
Rich Content
Schedules support text, image attachments, embeds, and pings to any user or role in your server.
🔑
Role-based Permissions
Grant specific roles the ability to create and manage schedules. Server admins always have full access.
🌍
Timezone Support
Configure your server's timezone so schedules fire at the right local time for your community.
🔢
Max-fire Limits
Cap how many times a schedule fires — perfect for limited-run announcements or countdown-style posts.
🔒
Privacy First
No message content snooping. The bot only stores what you explicitly schedule — nothing else.
Required permissions
PermissionStatusWhy it's needed
