SporkAlerts does not use Discord's privileged intents (Message Content, Server Members, Presence). It cannot read regular messages in your server.

What we store
DataWhy it exists
Server & channel IDsTo know where each schedule should post
Schedule contentThe message text and images you explicitly configure as scheduled posts
Timing configurationIntervals, target times, max-fire limits, and when a schedule last/next fires
Role & user IDsWho gets pinged and who's allowed to manage schedules
Server timezoneSo schedules fire at your local time

That's the whole list. No message snooping, no member lists, no presence tracking, no DMs, no analytics. Cancelled schedules and their images are deleted immediately. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

FAQ
Server admins always can. Beyond that, admins can grant configurator roles in /config setup — anyone with one of those roles can create, edit, and cancel schedules. Everyone else just sees the messages.
No. Run /sched create and an interactive wizard opens — dropdowns and buttons for the channel, timing, content, and pings, with a live summary before you confirm. No syntax to memorize.
Check your server's configured timezone — schedules fire based on it, not on each member's local time. Set it in /config setup or directly with /config timezone (it autocompletes as you type).
The bot needs the "Mention All Roles" permission to ping roles that aren't mentionable by everyone. Grant it in your server's role settings, or make the target role mentionable.
Schedules may be delayed or missed during outages, maintenance, or Discord platform issues. Configurations persist across restarts, so your schedules pick right back up — but for genuinely time-critical messages, keep a backup plan.
Cancelling a schedule from /sched list deletes its data and images immediately. To remove everything, remove the bot from your server and contact us via the support server — we'll purge any residual configuration.
